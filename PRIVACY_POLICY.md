## 2. Data We Collect
We collect only the data required to provide core functionality, keep the service stable, and understand how it is used.

### 2.1. Account Information
You can browse Qode without an account. To post, vote, or bookmark, you sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email. We store:
- Your email address
- An internal user ID
- Your display name and profile photo URL, if your Google account provides them
- When you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy

Your email address is never shown to other users. Your display name and profile photo are shown next to content you publish.

If you sign in with an email code, the code is sent to your address through our email provider (see Section 6).

### 2.2. User-Generated Content
- Promocodes and posts you submit, including titles, descriptions, tags, service names and websites
- Images you upload
- Your votes and bookmarks
- Counts of how many promocodes and posts you have submitted
- Timestamps (created/updated)

Promocodes, posts, uploaded images, and your display name and photo shown with them are public.

You can delete your own promocodes and posts. You can report content that is misleading, abusive, or illegal; reports are reviewed manually and content may be removed if it breaks the Terms of Service or applicable law.

Some deals are imported from public Telegram channels and published under a Qode account. These imports contain only the public deal text, not data about the people who posted them.

### 2.3. Service Popularity
When you pick a service as a filter, we add one to that service’s total pick count, which is used to order popular services. Only the total per service is stored; we do not record who picked it.

### 2.4. Reports and Blocking
When you report content, we store the reason, any details you add, the reported content’s ID, your user ID, and the time.

When you block a user, we store who you blocked and when. Their content is hidden from you, and you can unblock them at any time.

### 2.5. Analytics (Android app)
We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It may collect screen views, feature use, session length, and device information such as model, OS version, and app version.

Firebase Analytics is configured **without the Advertising ID**. We do no behavioral profiling and no ad personalization.

### 2.6. Crash Reports (Android app)
Firebase Crashlytics may collect your device model, OS version, app version, crash logs and stack traces, and a temporary IP address used for routing. This is used only to fix crashes.

### 2.7. Notifications (Android app)
If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a push token so notifications can be delivered. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

### 2.8. Website
The website stores your chosen language and theme in cookies, and your sign-in session in your browser’s local storage. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 3. Advertising & Promotional Content
Qode shows **non-personalized promotional banners**.

- Banners may promote Qode or third-party businesses that pay to be featured.
- Banners are the same for everyone; no tracking or personalization is used.

## 5. Permissions (Android app)

## 6. Service Providers
We use these providers to run Qode. They process data only to provide their service to us:
- **Supabase**: database, authentication, and image storage (servers in Frankfurt, Germany)
- **Resend**: delivery of email sign-in codes (servers in Ireland)
- **Google**: Google Sign-In, and Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging for the Android app
- **Cloudflare**: hosting of the website and email forwarding for qodein.com addresses
- **logo.dev**: service logos, which your browser or app loads by the service’s website address

No personal information is shared with advertisers or marketing partners.

## 7. Data Retention
We keep:
- Account data until you delete your account
- Content until you delete it, it is removed by moderation, or you delete your account
- Votes, bookmarks, and blocks until you change them or delete your account
- Reports until they are resolved
- Crash data according to Firebase’s retention settings
- Encrypted database backups for up to 14 days, after which deleted data is gone from backups too

---

## 8. Account Deletion
You can permanently delete your account from Settings in the Android app. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to **support@qodein.com** from the email address on the account.

Deleting your account removes:
- Your sign-in data (email, user ID, display name, profile photo)
- Your promocodes, posts, and uploaded images
- Your votes, bookmarks, blocks, and reports

**This is permanent and cannot be undone.** Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 14 days.
